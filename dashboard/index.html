Is it improving?

Validation AUC after each epoch. It stops after 3 epochs without a new best and keeps the best one. Grey line: your graded model. Hover for values.

Is it fitting the data?

% of bus arrivals over 3 min late, by hour. Dashed = what really happened, solid = predicted. Validation uses weeks it never trains on. Averages only, so a close match does not mean every prediction is right.

Can it tell late from on-time?

Each validation bus arrival gets a predicted chance of being late. The app warns for every bus right of the line. Less overlap between the hills = fewer mistakes (that is what AUC measures).
